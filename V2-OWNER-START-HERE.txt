AI VIDEO MAKER V2 - START HERE

Extract the entire ZIP before starting. Keep all folders together.
Node.js 18 or newer must already be installed. Nothing installs automatically.

1. Double-click START-AI-VIDEO-MAKER-V2.cmd.
2. Studio opens at http://127.0.0.1:8094/v2/.
   If the browser opens before startup finishes, reload the page.
   Run only one Studio copy at a time. Keep its terminal window open.
3. Enter your video idea. Optional: import your own audio or media.
4. Click Create video - FREE ONLY and wait for final verification.
5. Rights Review is intentionally required. Review your rights and the result.
   Publishing is always manual; this app never publishes automatically.

Generated media is stored in %USERPROFILE%\AIVM-V2-Media.
Final MP4 files are in its exports folder. An existing AIVM_MEDIA_ROOT or
AIVM_OUTPUT_ROOT environment setting overrides these default locations.
Projects and imported media are also stored in your browser. Keep using the
same browser and URL; do not clear its site data. Use Complete ZIP backups.

To stop: press Ctrl+C in the launcher terminal; confirm if Windows asks.
Close the terminal after the services stop.

The included local fallback creates draft stills and FFmpeg camera motion.
Advanced AI image/video models are future upgrades, not required for this
fallback production. No paid provider, model download or admin access is needed.
